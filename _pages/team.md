**Principal Investigator**

__Jenny Bigman__ is a quantitative ecologist and an Assistant Professor in the School of Marine Science and Policy at the University of Delaware. Her group is based at the Hugh R. Sharp campus in Lewes, DE. Prior to joining UD, Jenny was a postdoctoral fellow and Fish Biologist at NOAA Fisheries. She combines macroecology and fisheries science to answer applied questions and advance ecological theory. Please see [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=QV_j91EAAAAJ&hl=en) for an updated list of publications, her [CV](/files/Bigman-CV-Sep2025.pdf), and profile on the [UDEL website](https://www.udel.edu/academics/colleges/ceoe/departments/smsp/faculty/jennifer-bigman/).
