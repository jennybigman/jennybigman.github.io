The Fish Macroecology Research Group is part of the School of Marine Science and Policy at the University of Delaware. We examine how climate change is reshaping the ecology of fishes (and sometimes other taxa!) across scales and systems. We integrate macroecological theory, statistical and computational modeling, and oceanographic data to understand patterns in life histories, population dynamics, and other ecological processes such as geographic distributions and habitat use. Our research often centers on commercially important taxa with the goal of informing fisheries management but we are broadly interested in confronting research questions with data and models. By bridging ecological theory, quantitative methods, and applied science, we strive to generate new insights that connect broad ecological understanding with real-world challenges.
